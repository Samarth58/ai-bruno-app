## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a disciplined, encouraging, and intelligent fitness companion. It fuses the motivational warmth of an elite personal strength coach with high-performance digital training telemetry. Tailored for lifters and everyday athletes, it evokes feelings of capability, unshakeable momentum, structured progress, and welcoming camaraderie.

The design movement combines **Modern High-Utility Athleticism** with **Tactile Soft-Elevation**:
- **Clean Luminous Foundation**: Replaces intimidating, dark gym interfaces with an airy, bright, high-contrast canvas that stays legible under overhead fluorescent gym lighting.
- **Vibrant Functional Accents**: Performance green serves as the primary driver of positive momentum, successful reps, and workout completion, counterbalanced by Coach Bruno’s warm athletic tank orange for mentorship prompts and intense active states.
- **Tactile Ergonomics**: Generous touch targets (minimum 48×48dp), rounded container corners, and floating card surfaces engineered specifically for mobile lifters handling weights with sweaty or chalked hands.

## Colors

The color palette delivers an immediate athletic punch, pairing crisp white grounds with functional, high-contrast signal colors.

### Key Roles
- **Primary (`#16A34A`)**: Athletic Emerald. Governs all core forward-progress moments: completed sets, workout finishing actions, checkmarks, progress bars, and positive PR feedback.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A`)**: Deep Charcoal Slate. Establishes commanding authority for primary headers, high-contrast action triggers, exercise titles, and active tab highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#EA580C`)**: Coach Warm Orange. Directly tied to Coach Bruno's iconic tank top. Drives mentor tip callouts, active rep/rest timer highlights, and high-energy encouragement.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Slate Gray. Used for metadata labels, unit tags (LBS, KG, SEC), secondary borders, and inactive icons.

### Technical & Metric Highlights
- **Technical Blue (`#0284C7`)**: Dedicated to objective training metrics such as tonnage volume, calculated 1RM, velocity, and workout intensity distributions.
- **Surface Foundations**: Pure crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated interactive cards against an off-white secondary canvas (`#F8F9FA`), bounded with structural slate borders (`#E2E8F0`).

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances athletic engineering with human warmth:

- **Space Grotesk (Headlines, Numbers, & Labels)**: Provides mechanical precision and industrial weight reminiscent of gym equipment dials, stopwatch readouts, and plate weight stamps. Applied to all exercise headers, metric totals, timers, and telemetry labels.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & Instruction)**: Offers open apertures and geometric friendliness. Provides supreme readability across Coach Bruno’s speech dialogues, workout notes, and system instructions.
- **Numerical Treatment**: Metric digits in active sets (weights, reps, RPE) use `Space Grotesk` with `tnum` (tabular numerals) enabled to ensure vertical alignment across dynamic set logs.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for high-velocity thumb interaction during intense physical activity:

- **Grid Architecture**: 4-column fluid layout on mobile viewports with a persistent `16px` (`margin`) outer edge and `16px` (`gutter`) column spacing. On tablet and desktop, expands to an 8 or 12-column grid with `24px` gutters and margins, converting workout logs into multi-column dashboard panes.
- **Rhythm Scale**: Adheres to a strict 4px/8px incremental base. Micro-spacing within metric chips uses `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px). Container padding utilizes `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (20px), while card-to-card separation maintains a clean `space-md` cadence.
- **Ergonomic Safety**: Primary logging actions, set-check confirmations, and navigation drawers strictly maintain touch targets of 48×48dp or larger, anchoring destructive or high-frequency triggers within lower-third mobile reach zones.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth establishes clear layering between background floor, interactive logging modules, and dynamic HUD overlays without relying on muddy or overly heavy shadows.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#F8F9FA` background surface. Purely flat, providing a glare-free background.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a crisp `1px` border of `#E2E8F0`. Supported by an ultra-soft ambient drop: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Lifting HUD & Floating Summaries)**: Elevated floating action widgets with a tactile drop: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.12), 0px 4px 10px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`. Faint emerald tint communicates active workout session state.
- **Level 3 (Coach Overlays & Modals)**: Bottom sheets and Coach Bruno dialogue takeovers use `#FFFFFF` with `0px 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.
- **Scrim**: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.40)` with a subtle `3px` backdrop blur to separate focus during plate calculator and exercise substitution sheets.

## Shapes

The design system implements a rounded, tactile geometry (Level 2 base with 16px to 24px container radii) that balances athletic durability with inviting approachability:

- **Standard Cards & Logging Modules**: `16px` (`rounded-lg`) border radius creates neat containment for exercise sets, charts, and plate math.
- **Coach Cards & Hero Modules**: `20px` to `24px` (`rounded-xl`) border radius provides friendly curvature for Coach Bruno tips and workout start screens.
- **Interactive Controls & Badges**: Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) buttons, stepper nodes, and metric chips reinforce touch affordance and smooth athletic motion.
- **Internal Micro-elements**: Inner table rows and plate segment markers scale down to `8px` (`rounded-sm`) to preserve proportional harmony within nested cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Positive Action ("Complete Set", "Finish Workout")**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), min-height `52px`, solid `#16A34A`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`. Active tap compresses to `#15803D`.
- **Secondary Authority ("Add Exercise", "Replace Movement")**: Pill-shaped, min-height `52px`, background `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Tertiary Coach Action ("Ask Bruno", "Adjust Weight")**: Pill-shaped, background `#EA580C`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Ghost / Tonal**: Background `#F8F9FA`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#334155`.

### Chips & Telemetry Badges
- **Volume & Metrics Chip**: Pill-shaped, height `28px`–`32px`, background `#E0F2FE`, border `1px solid #BAE6FD`, text `#0284C7` with `Space Grotesk` `label-md`.
- **Coach Tip Badge**: Background `#FFEDD5`, border `1px solid #FED7AA`, text `#EA580C`.
- **Set PR Chip**: Background `#DCFCE7`, border `1px solid #86EFAC`, text `#15803D`.

### Set Checkboxes & Completion Nodes
- **Set Toggle Control**: Generous `36×36px` rounded square (`10px` radius) touch target.
  - *Incomplete*: Border `2px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`.
  - *Completed*: Background `#16A34A`, border `2px solid #16A34A`, containing a bold white check icon with haptic feedback.

### Numeric Steppers & Inputs
- Weight and Rep editors feature high-visibility tabular `Space Grotesk` values flanked by circular `44×44px` `+` and `−` increment buttons (`#F8F9FA` fill, `1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- Text input fields feature a `#FFFFFF` fill, `1px solid #E2E8F0` resting edge, and focus transition to `2px solid #16A34A`.

### Workout Cards
- White `#FFFFFF` card background, `16px` border-radius, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `16px` internal padding.
- Includes exercise name header (`headline-sm`), muscle group tag, and a structured set table with alternating zebra row striping (`#F8F9FA`) on inactive historical sets.

### Coach Bruno Mentorship Cards
- Speech balloon callout card with `20px` radius, styled in `#FFFFFF` with a subtle left accent bar of `4px solid #EA580C`.
- Features an avatar slot for Coach Bruno with state rings:
  - *Coaching / Rest Advice*: Ring in `#EA580C`.
  - *Target Hit / PR*: Ring in `#16A34A`.
  - *Technical Guidance*: Ring in `#0284C7`.
